Questions

Answers before you spend anything.

If something here isn't clear, ask us — you'll get a straight answer, including "no."

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The 7-day trial takes an email address and nothing else. There is no card on file, so there is nothing to auto-bill — a trial only becomes a subscription if you choose a plan yourself.

How fast do I get access?

Your access code is issued by hand, normally within one business day. Every trial is set up individually rather than by a drip campaign, so a real person sees your request. If you need it faster for a specific deal, say so and we'll move.

Does Maco give me an ARV for each property?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. Florida counties do not publish a live sold-comp feed, so there is no per-property ARV in the product. What you get is a ZIP-level sale band built from real recorded arm's-length sales, plus the property's tax-roll size and vintage. Use the band to shortlist, then walk the property and comp it properly before you offer. The built-in underwriter runs the full flip P&L on your numbers.

Which counties are live, and is the data equally deep?

Six: Miami-Dade, Broward, Lee, Collier, Polk and Lake. Depth is not equal — Miami-Dade is deepest, with code cases, permits and recorded liens joined per parcel. Other counties carry owner, hold and mailing signals, with code/lien depth added county by county. The markets page shows each county's actual snapshot date and lead count, generated from the data itself.

How current is the data?

It's a dated snapshot, not a live query, and the date is shown in the product and on the markets page. Counties are refreshed on a rotation. When a county is still on its annual tax roll rather than a dated refresh, the site says exactly that instead of implying it is fresh.

What's the difference between the plans?

Founding Member ($50) is launch pricing — the rate holds for as long as you stay continuously subscribed. Standard Access ($75) is full access in the primary supported market. Market Select ($85) lets you pick the one supported market you actually invest in, with additional markets at +$10/month each. Every plan starts with the same 7-day trial.

Can I cancel myself?

Yes. Manage your subscription in the Stripe customer portal — cancel, update a card or pull invoices without emailing anyone. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you've already paid for, and your first month is refundable within 7 days of the first charge.

How is this different from PropStream or DealMachine?

They're nationwide and shallow; this is six Florida counties and deep — county code cases, recorded liens and estates read from the counties' own files rather than a resold national list. Full side-by-side on the comparison page.

Is this investment advice?

No. Everything in the product is an estimate derived from public records, provided for your own analysis. Verify independently — including title, liens and condition — before acting on any of it. See disclosures.
